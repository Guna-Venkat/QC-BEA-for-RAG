# LLMLingua-2 paper claims — literature only

Source: [ACL Findings2024 paper](paper/llmlingua2_acl_findings_2024.pdf), abstract on physical page1; [official publication](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.57/).

The paper reports compression-method speedups of3×–6× and end-to-end speedups of1.6×–2.9× at2×–5×compression ratios in its experiments. These are not measurements on our server and must not be reused as project efficiency results.

The project qualified the official smaller mBERT checkpoint, not an automatic replication of the paper’s XLM-R-large results. Qualification alone is not a full quality experiment; the canonical W12 results do not contain an evaluated LLMLINGUA2 arm.
