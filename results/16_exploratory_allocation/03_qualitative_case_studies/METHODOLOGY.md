# Retrospective diagnostic definitions

Retrospective, outcome-selected explanatory case studies, not confirmation or population failure frequencies. Gold annotations are diagnostic only and were not available to the allocator. No allocation, predictor, threshold or answer is changed. The BEA_V2 preregistered calibration remains CALIBRATION_FAILED_TERMINATED; the V2 answers come from the separately labelled post-hoc tau=8e-5 study.

Gold evidence recall = fraction of mapped, deduplicated annotations whose non-whitespace source content is fully covered by selected units (best alternative occurrence for repeated QASPER text). Gold parent recall = fraction of annotated native documents represented, not coarse-chunk necessity. Gold token coverage = fraction of non-whitespace tokenizer-offset gold tokens whose content is fully covered; whitespace-only gaps between exact selected source sentences are ignored, not counted as lost evidence. Unavailable if the pinned local tokenizer cannot provide offsets. Annotation pooling is not a minimal necessary-evidence set. Unmapped annotations are excluded from computable fractions and reported separately; absent NEI support produces unavailable recall, not 0 or 1. Retrieval classifications use the same annotations, and unavailable/incomplete mappings are separate statuses.

Fragmentation diagnostics: contiguous selected runs within each coarse parent, adjacent selected-sentence pairs / exposed underlying sentences, underlying sentences / represented parent, and source switches in saved transition order where available. FULL expands to all underlying source sentences for diagnostics. These are operational proxies, not semantic coherence judgments.

Automatic mechanism labels remain CANDIDATE. OBSERVED only denotes direct mapped-source overlap or invalid parsed output. No redundancy-overpenalization, context-dilution or P_help-miscalibration label is inferred automatically from aggregate correlation. Reviewers may add supported labels, with exact source/trace references and rationale. MANUAL_REVIEW.csv is never overwritten. Counts report selected-case slots and unique query/budget units; they are not population estimates. All paired outcome retention strata use per-query QA F1 or SciFact correctness; aggregate SciFact quality remains macro-F1.

No GPU, model loading, inference, allocation, embedding, tuning or answer regeneration occurs. A tokenizer is loaded locally on CPU for exact saved-context verification and token offsets. The notebook never launches missing study stages.
