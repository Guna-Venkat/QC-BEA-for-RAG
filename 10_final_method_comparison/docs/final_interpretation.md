# Interpretation

Point estimates are task-dependent. MiniLM is highly competitive on SciFact, FIXED/QBGF are close in QASPER, and static reranking does not reproduce the strongest post-hoc V2 Hotpot estimates. These are previously analyzed development cohorts, small and exploratory. No MiniLM comparison passes its saved 24-family Holm correction. Original W12 final policy remains FIXED_SENTENCE; no exploratory result revises it.
